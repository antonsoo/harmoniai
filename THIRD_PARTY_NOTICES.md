# Third-party material

- **Fonts.** GFS Neohellenic (Greek Font Society), Gentium Book Plus (SIL
  International) and Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit (Tiro Typeworks, the Indigo Project), served from `src/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; full
  notices in `src/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.
- **Cleonides.** K. von Jan, *Musici scriptores Graeci* (Leipzig: Teubner, 1895), public
  domain. Read from the Internet Archive scan `musiciscriptoresjank`; its PDF had SHA-256
  `a442b3d4bbcf3ab6a8a0872f68ebc8a90e891985bf3fa40f4716767c02fb58e8` when consulted.
  Complete page images 197–199 are retained unchanged in `public/sources/` with
  their original URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes in `manifest.json`. Image
  indices 298–300 were verified by reading the printed page numbers; the scan's
  metadata leaf numbering is offset from the image endpoint. The short new
  diatonic transcriptions normalize initial capitalization and apostrophes, and
  mark omissions. English translations are new.
- **Modern mode names.** Interval orders referenced to *Steps to Music Theory*,
  chapter 11.1, Texas A&M University Libraries Open Digital Publishing:
  https://odp.library.tamu.edu/stepstomusictheory/chapter/modes/ . No score images
  or recordings are copied.
- **Plato and Aristotle.** Greek text of Burnet's *Republic* (1903) and Ross's *Politics*
  (1957) as published by the Perseus Digital Library, © Tufts University, CC BY-SA 3.0 US.
  The short quotations are shared under the same license.
- **Dictionary glosses.** Short quotations from Liddell, Scott and Jones, *A
  Greek–English Lexicon* (9th ed.), through Logeion (University of Chicago).
- **Coastlines.** Natural Earth 1:50m land (naturalearthdata.com), public domain, clipped
  and simplified by `scripts/aegean.py` into `src/content/aegean.ts`.
- **Nāṭyaśāstra.** Ed. Śivadatta and K. P. Parab, Kāvyamālā 42 (Bombay: Nirṇaya Sāgara
  Press, 1894), public domain. Read from the Internet Archive scan
  `natyasastrakasinathpandurangparabpanditsivadattanirnayasagarpresskm42mula`; its PDF had
  SHA-256 `d05c869a28d6595c079702a820bfc2e84f87bfec47bf970d7d2688fb4f20f070` when consulted.
  Its chapter 28 is taken over from J. Grosset, *Contribution à l'étude de la musique hindoue*
  (Paris, 1888), public domain, whose pp. 21, 28–29 and 86 were also consulted. Two words of
  M. Ghosh's 1961 translation are quoted for comment.
- **Sanskrit glosses.** Short quotations from M. Monier-Williams, *A Sanskrit-English
  Dictionary* (Oxford, 1899), public domain, through the Cologne Digital Sanskrit
  Dictionaries (Universität zu Köln).
