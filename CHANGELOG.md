# Changelog

## Unreleased

- `vina.html`: Bharata's two-vīṇā demonstration of the twenty-two śrutis on a dial, with
  the Sanskrit of Nāṭyaśāstra 28 from the 1894 Kāvyamālā edition, the fourteen
  mūrcchanās beside Cleonides' octave species, and fifteen Sanskrit words.
- A step of the plucked-string player can sound several strings together.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)

- Fifteen strings of the Greater Perfect System, named and glossed, plucked in the browser.
- Cleonides' seven octave species with his Greek, both of his descriptions, and the
  modern names of the same octaves.
- Plato and Aristotle on the harmoniai; a word list; a find-the-string drill.
